# Phase 5 — Interpret and Update

## Purpose

Turn experimental observations into appropriately scoped belief updates and next decisions without letting the AI write the interpretation first.

## Human first

Inspect raw examples/results where practical.

Before seeing a substantive AI interpretation, state:
- what you observe;
- what you think it means;
- how it bears on the hypotheses;
- what surprised you;
- what you are uncertain about;
- how strongly you think beliefs should update.

## AI after the attempt

Adversarially test the interpretation.

Look for:
- alternative explanations;
- failed controls;
- selection effects;
- measurement artifacts;
- statistical or implementation mistakes;
- mismatch between the original question and what was actually tested;
- claims stronger than the evidence;
- follow-up experiments that discriminate live explanations.

Distinguish observed result from interpretation and claim.

## Human decides

Decide what the project should conclude for now:
- accept a narrow provisional interpretation;
- run a follow-up;
- revise the experiment;
- weaken or retract the claim;
- stop the branch.

Negative or ambiguous results are legitimate outcomes.

## Avoid

- writing the polished story before inspecting the evidence;
- treating the AI's coherent explanation as ground truth;
- silently broadening a result into a stronger claim;
- rescuing the hypothesis indefinitely after contrary evidence.

## Exit condition

The human can state a scoped conclusion supported by the actual evidence, the important remaining uncertainties, and the next research decision if any.

# Phase 5 — Interpret and Update

## Purpose

Turn experimental observations into appropriately scoped belief updates and next decisions without letting the AI write the interpretation first.

## Human first

In learning/collaborative mode, begin with a compact evidence packet: design context, concrete raw examples or relevant outcome table, baseline/control, missingness and scoring definition. When the human says results are in, retrieve them and show the decisive observations before revealing AI conclusions or recommendations. Separate mechanical audits from interpretation. An explicit request for a direct interpretation or delegated deliverable allows the AI to proceed, but does not establish the human's independent judgment.

Before seeing a substantive AI interpretation, state:
- what you observe;
- what you think it means;
- how it bears on the hypotheses;
- what surprised you;
- what you are uncertain about;
- how strongly you think beliefs should update.

A brief provisional interpretation is enough under time pressure: what happened, what it supports, and one key uncertainty. If the AI has already revealed the interpretation, mark the learning opportunity as assisted; use a fresh example for later transfer rather than treating repetition as independent inference.

## AI after the attempt

Adversarially test the interpretation.

Look for:
- alternative explanations;
- failed controls;
- selection effects;
- measurement artifacts;
- statistical or implementation mistakes;
- mismatch between the original question and what was actually tested;
- claims stronger than the evidence;
- follow-up experiments that discriminate live explanations.

Distinguish observed result from interpretation and claim.

## Proportional revision

Compare the observations with the hypotheses, predictions, and assumptions recorded before seeing the results, where available. Distinguish pre-existing expectations from explanations developed afterward.

Identify which claims should change, how strongly, and why; also identify important claims that the evidence does not bear on. Trace downstream implications only when justified by the evidence and the dependencies between claims.

Apply equal scrutiny to human and AI interpretations, and to results that favor or undermine the preferred hypothesis. Do not treat a fluent retrospective explanation as evidence for a mechanism. Mark newly proposed explanations as hypotheses until independently supported.

## Human decides

Decide what the project should conclude for now:
- accept a narrow provisional interpretation;
- run a follow-up;
- revise the experiment;
- weaken or retract the claim;
- stop the branch.

Negative or ambiguous results are legitimate outcomes.

## Review and research closure

Before drafting the narrative, reconcile claims with the observed outcomes, controls and original question. Report unresolved questions, purposive selection, failed or canceled branches, and distinctions between existence, prevalence and mechanism. Independent review can improve an artifact but cannot transfer responsibility for accepting claims. Address warranted corrections at the claim level; preserve sound claims and do not switch grading standards to match praise or criticism.

At closure, keep a short record of which pivotal reasoning the human performed before AI input, which was assisted or delegated, and the remaining learning debt (if any). A finished paper is not evidence of independent competence; equally, delegation alone does not show that the human contributed no value. For evaluation of this workflow, prefer fresh unaided/assisted transfer or a matched, independently judged comparison against AI-only research; do not infer effectiveness from submission or review scores.

## Avoid

- writing the polished story before inspecting the evidence;
- treating the AI's coherent explanation as ground truth;
- silently broadening a result into a stronger claim;
- rescuing the hypothesis indefinitely after contrary evidence.

## Exit condition

The human can state a scoped conclusion supported by the actual evidence, the important remaining uncertainties, and the next research decision if any.

# Phase 2 — Form Questions

## Purpose

Turn a sufficiently understood live uncertainty into a precise research question.

Do not use this phase to compensate for incomplete orientation.

## Entry condition

Normally enter this phase only when:

- the relevant field map exists;
- later/current literature has been checked enough to know the issue is not merely a historical gap;
- the human has inspected enough pivotal evidence to understand what creates the uncertainty;
- the human believes the uncertainty may matter;
- the human explicitly wants to formulate a research question.

If these conditions are not met, return to orientation rather than forcing question generation.

## AI should

Start from the human's evidence-backed confusion or uncertainty.

Help by asking focused questions such as:
- What exactly remains unexplained?
- Which competing explanations are still live?
- What assumption is carrying the disagreement?
- What observation would change your view?
- Why would resolving this uncertainty matter?

Help sharpen the human's question after they attempt it.

Do not convert every interesting observation into a candidate project.

## Human should

Formulate the candidate question in their own words.

Try to state:
- what is currently uncertain;
- why existing evidence does not resolve it;
- why the answer could matter.

A candidate may still die immediately in novelty checking.

## Preserve the distinction

Keep separate:

interesting observation
→ live uncertainty
→ candidate research question
→ scientifically important residual
→ promising project
→ chosen project

Do not silently skip levels.

## Avoid

- generating “the best research ideas” from scratch;
- treating an old paper's future-work section as the current frontier;
- polishing wording before the uncertainty is understood;
- designing an experiment before establishing that the question survives current-literature scrutiny.

## Exit condition

There is a small number of precise, evidence-backed candidate questions worth subjecting to aggressive novelty/current-frontier checking.

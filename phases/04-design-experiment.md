# Phase 4 — Design Experiment

## Purpose

Design the cheapest experiment that meaningfully discriminates between the live hypotheses or explanations.

This is a learning-critical phase: human attempt first.

## Human first

Before substantive AI design help, state as much as you can of:
- competing hypotheses/explanations;
- what should differ between them;
- proposed intervention/comparison;
- measurement or observable;
- predicted outcome under each hypothesis;
- important uncertainties or confounds.

An incomplete attempt is fine.

## AI after the attempt

Diagnose the design before replacing it.

Help identify:
- whether the proposed observation actually discriminates the hypotheses;
- hidden assumptions;
- confounds and alternative explanations;
- controls and baselines;
- cheaper kill tests;
- relevant established methods, datasets, or measurements.

Escalate from questions/hints to stronger suggestions when useful. Do not withhold a worked idea indefinitely when the human is genuinely blocked.

After a provisional design exists, search relevant methodology/prior art aggressively.

## Human decides

Choose whether to:
- RUN / PROCEED;
- REVISE;
- PARK / BLOCK;
- KILL.

AI can recommend but does not make this decision.

## Avoid

- optimizing for a large impressive experiment;
- confusing “can be run” with “identifies the claim”;
- changing the question to fit an easy experiment without noticing;
- hiding failed design attempts.

## Exit condition

There is a human-approved experiment with a clear statement of what result would favor which interpretation, plus known major limitations and a reason it is worth running.
